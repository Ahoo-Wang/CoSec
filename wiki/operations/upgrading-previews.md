# Upgrading Between 8.0 Previews {#between-8-0-previews}

The upgrade notes between the 8.0 pre-releases (the alphas, betas and release candidates of 8.0.0) were removed from
this site when 8.0.0 was released. They are kept as they stood at the last candidate:
[Upgrading between 8.0 previews, as of 8.0.0-rc.2](https://github.com/Ahoo-Wang/CoSec/blob/v8.0.0-rc.2/wiki/operations/upgrading-previews.md).
To upgrade a CoSec 7.x deployment, read [Upgrading](./upgrading.md). From `8.0.0-rc.2` (or `8.0.0-rc.1`) to `8.0.0`, read the
[release notes of 8.0.0](https://github.com/Ahoo-Wang/CoSec/releases/tag/v8.0.0).
